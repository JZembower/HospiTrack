About HospiTrack

Making emergency healthcare access faster, smarter, and more informed

Our Mission

HospiTrack is designed to help people find the most appropriate emergency care facility based on their specific medical needs and location. In critical moments, having the right information can make all the difference. We combine hospital quality data, wait times, and specialized care capabilities to help you make informed decisions about where to seek emergency medical treatment.

What is HospiTrack?

HospiTrack is an intelligent hospital search and recommendation platform that helps you:

  • Find nearby hospitals based on your current location or any address
  • Compare emergency departments by wait times, quality ratings, and patient outcomes
  • Get specialized recommendations for specific medical conditions (heart attack, stroke, trauma, etc.)
  • Make informed decisions with data-driven insights on hospital performance

Our platform aggregates data from thousands of hospitals across the United States, providing you with comprehensive, up-to-date information when you need it most.

Who Is This For?

👥 Patients & Families

Individuals seeking emergency care who want to choose the best hospital for their specific condition, minimizing wait times and maximizing quality of care.

🚑 Emergency Responders

EMS professionals and first responders who need quick access to hospital capabilities and availability for efficient patient routing.

🏠 Travelers & Relocators

People traveling or moving to new areas who want to familiarize themselves with nearby emergency care options before an urgent need arises.

📊 Healthcare Researchers

Analysts and researchers studying emergency care patterns, hospital performance, and healthcare access across different regions.

How It Works

1

Enter Your Location

Provide your address, coordinates, or use your device's location services to find nearby hospitals.

2

Select Your Needs

Choose your medical condition or symptom, and specify what's most important to you (speed, quality, distance).

3

Get Recommendations

View ranked hospitals based on your specific needs, complete with wait times, quality scores, and specialized care data.

4

Make an Informed Decision

Compare options on our interactive map and list view, then choose the best hospital for your situation.

Technology & Data

HospiTrack is built with modern web technologies to provide a fast, reliable, and user-friendly experience:

  • FastAPI Backend for high-performance API responses
  • Real-time Geocoding for accurate location-based searches
  • Comprehensive Hospital Database with 4,000+ US hospitals
  • Interactive Mapping powered by Leaflet
  • Responsive Design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

Our data comes from publicly available hospital information, including Medicare quality ratings, emergency department performance metrics, and hospital capabilities.

Privacy & Safety

Your privacy and safety are our priorities:

  • We do not store your personal health information or search history

  • We do not share your data with third parties
